🔧 Machine Guidance

Shaka's canon (7/28): "just as the Admiral is a machine guiding me, Jarred is the Machine guiding our build." This window is The Machine's shrine — the systems he taught us, written down so any turtle can run them. 🔓 Consent consensus granted 2026-08-01 (declared by Shaka): the filmed lessons are live — watch them below or in the ShellPit's Machine window. 🛡️

🏷️ The Naming + the shower RUNS ⚡ The Trailer, Machinified 💧 The Water Loop, end to end

🎥 Full lessons: The Naming (19s) · Trailer walkthrough (119s) · Water loop (150s) · b-roll (28s) — karaoke transcripts in the pit

⚡ The Trailer, Machinified taught 7/28

  1. Transfer switch is the heart. Labeled positions: panel = running on panel power off the channel; battery = flip to battery, make sure it's on, lights come on.
  2. Generator kill: if power is done or something bad happens with Jenny, kill it and click over to wall power.
  3. 16 outlets throughout the entire trailer — plug anything in. 30 amps on both sides.
  4. Charge points: USB + USB-C on the panel. This little house can be a whole house. 🐢

💧 The Water Loop, End to End taught 7/28

  1. Hookup: wrap threads with tape → screw on the coupler → tighten with channel locks → slide the cam-lock on.
  2. Prime: plug in — the pump starts immediately. Water flows through the orange line to the transfer pump.
  3. To the kitchen: transfer pump pushes water all the way around to the kitchen.
  4. Foot switch: Jeremy's handy-dandy foot switch, jumped into the greywater line — step on it to pump.
  5. Float-switch chain: greywater tank fills → float switch triggers the sump pump → water moves to the shower → shower basin fills → its float switch triggers → everything pumps back to the greywater tank.

🧠 The Mind of The Machine: "Is the pump always on? How does it interact with valves at each termination point?" — from the 7/16 David sync, before he touched a single weld. Twelve days later, the shower ran.
