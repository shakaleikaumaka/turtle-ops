PART 3 · WATER

💧 The Water Loop, End to End

IMG_4626 · 2026-07-28 · 150.2s · feat. Victor "Victory!"

A complete runbook, taught slow on purpose — for the day Jeremy gets back and wants to hook the water up and test everything:

  1. Wrap the threads with thread-seal tape; screw on the fitting.
  2. Tighten with channel locks.
  3. Slide the cam-lock on.
  4. Plug in — you can hear the pump start immediately.
  5. Water runs through the orange line to the transfer pump — and all the way around to the kitchen.
  6. Jeremy's foot switch (jumped into the greywater line) → greywater tank fills to the float switch → sump pump activates → pumps to the shower.
  7. The shower basin's float switch → all the water pumps back to the greywater tank. Zero spills.
