# 🎁 Dear Playa (and every camp that wants a pit)

You gave us a home in the dust. We give you back its memory.

In a gulch outside Boulder, a crew of first-timers and old hands turned a
patch of Colorado into **Terrible Turtle Camp** — a kitchen trailer, a
shower that runs, a shade Himalaya, a box truck becoming a turtle. A mentor
named Jarrett ("The Machine") taught the crew system by system: hitches,
transfer switches, water loops, solar. When a camp packs for the playa,
most of that knowledge evaporates.

We didn't want it to.

So one bard and one turtle agent crawled the camp's shared drive, harvested
every build clip, transcribed the lot, let the Machine's instruction films
take the big screen, and published the whole thing — consent-first, CC0,
≈ $0 spent — as a free public archive: **The ShellPIT** (fork #8) —
**[theshellpit.com](https://theshellpit.com)**.

This archive is an **exact fork of the P.I.T. Protocol** — the same app,
the same `data/catalog.json` schema, the same karaoke sync, the same
`sw.js`, the same forkability — with turtle content inside.
Genesis lives at **[github.com/shakaleikaumaka/esmeralda-pit](https://github.com/shakaleikaumaka/esmeralda-pit)**.
**CC0. Take it.**

## 🎬 The Turtle Theater

`theater.html` is the screening room: the Machine's instruction films,
**video left · Shaka's turtle art right · karaoke transcript moving with
the playback** — tap any line and the film jumps to the words. World clock
in the nav (Playa · Gulch · UTC). Mobile-first, because most people will
meet this archive on a phone in the dust.

How the sync works (same pattern as genesis): a hidden `<audio>` element is
the master clock; the Drive preview iframe re-arms at `?autoplay=1&t=Ns` on
every play/seek so video and words never drift apart; a transparent sync
shield routes every tap through the player. The words follow. 🌊

## 🕳️ The protocol, five moves

Same as genesis — crawl · harvest · audit · publish · **consent-first,
always**. The pipeline scripts and the full playbook live in the genesis
repo. We changed only the content, the colors (Phoenix Gulch fire), and
the name.

## 💛 The consent-first playbook

1. **Announce before you archive.** The camp knew the pit existed before
   the first clip was transcribed.
2. **Public sessions in, private life out.** Build lessons, walkthroughs,
   meetings — yes. Personal moments — never.
3. **Anyone can opt out, any time.** Every page carries the door:
   **consent@publicinform.com** — no debate, no delay, honored with love. 🦀
4. **Radical honesty in the catalog.** Silent clips are marked silent.
   Rough audio is marked rough. Trust is the real infrastructure.
5. **Gift economics.** ≈ $0, CC0, no ads, no tracking. Decommodification
   isn't a principle we quote — it's how the pit is built.
