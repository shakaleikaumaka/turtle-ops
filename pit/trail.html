PART 5 · POWER · NEW 8/1

🔌 The Power Curriculum — solar, generator, grid

IMG_4710 / 4711 / 4715 / 4716 · 2026-08-01 party-eve session · ~11 min total · 🎬 watch in the Turtle Theater

The evening before the party, the trailer got its full power curriculum — three independent ways to keep the lights on and the food prepped. A masterclass in redundancy:

  • Solar generator (Anker SOLIX) — the trailer-to-generator attachment cable runs to the 4-prong transfer switch. Panel power vs battery, main switches on, battery switch OFF unless needed. "This generator is going to run this panel."
  • Predator gas generator (fallback) — kill the solar, transfer switch off, four-prong straight into the Predator's red receptacle, twist-lock. "You just start it and plug it in. It's ready to go."
  • David's camp grid — the cable labeled in tape "David power to kitchen": 70A camp panel split into four duplex boxes, runs off a regular plug. Simulated live with the Anker's AC button.
  • The battery drill — power out? Flip to battery, light comes on, keep prepping food. "These are repeatable systems — so we have backups in a worst case… we can still meet our obligations to the camp."
  • And when it doesn't work? — "I don't know why. This is why we troubleshoot." The curriculum includes the debugging, on camera.
"We can charge our batteries and make sure we have some reserve before we leave — so these lights work, so we can still prep food and still meet our obligations to the camp."

Teacher confirmed: The Machine — IMG_4709 opens with "We got The Machine leading the team." Same session, same teacher, full curriculum. 🐢
