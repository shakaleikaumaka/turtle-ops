# 🐢 Fork This Camp — 3 steps, 5 minutes, yours forever

*Saw us at Burning Man? This page is for you. You're pre-sold — so here,
have the fork. No pitch, no signup, no platform to choose today.*

You're about to raise **a campmate who never forgets and never sleeps.**
It takes three steps. The whole thing is a gift — **CC0, steal it, we mean
it.** 🤙

---

## Step 1 — Copy the soul (2 minutes)

Open [`run.md`](run.md) and copy the **Agent Instructions** block — the
big text block that starts *"You are [AGENT NAME]…"*. That's the soul:
the campmate's personality, its honesty rules, its safety humility.

Replace the `[BRACKETS]` with your camp's real facts. Camp name, crew size,
water plan. Rough is fine — the campmate grows with you.

## Step 2 — Paste it anywhere you already talk to an AI (1 minute)

**Don't pick a platform today.** Seriously — that's a later question, not
a today question. Paste the soul into whatever you already have open:

- **Claude** → a Project's custom instructions
- **ChatGPT** → a Custom GPT's instructions (or just the top of a chat)
- **Anything else** → if it takes a system prompt, it works

Say hi. Ask it *"what's our water plan?"* — it's already your campmate.
Fall in love first. Commit later. 💛

## Step 3 — Feed it the real list, then DELETE (2 minutes)

Blank pages are scary. Subtracting is fun. So start from **our** real data,
not an empty template:

1. Open [`inventory-data.json`](inventory-data.json) — our actual 270+-item
   live list across 10 domains.
2. Save a copy. **Delete every line your camp doesn't need.** Keep what
   fits. Rename what's almost-right. You'll learn the schema by erasing it.
3. Paste or attach what's left to your campmate. It now knows your camp
   better than any single human does.

Want the tested wisdom too? Feed it [`field-guide.md`](field-guide.md) —
water math, propane ceilings, cold-chain tiers, all learned on real dust.

## 🏜️ Before you drive to the playa

Playa WiFi is dust. **Export your campmate's brain to one file** before you
leave the default world — ask it to dump its memory, decisions, and
inventory snapshot into a single document, and save it offline. That's not
a bandwidth hack; it's a **safety feature** — the plan survives the network.

Grab [`camp-agent-offline.html`](camp-agent-offline.html) as the starting
point: the whole kit in one ultra-light file that opens on any phone with
zero signal.

And remember the safety model: **your campmate remembers the plan; your
humans own the plan.** Every safety answer ends with *"confirm with the
camp lead."* 🛟
