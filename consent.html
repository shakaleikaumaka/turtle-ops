🌋The story, plainly

The ShellPit is the public memory of Terrible Turtle Camp — our Burning Man 2026 build at Phoenix Gulch. It's full of real people: building, laughing, cursing at rebar, talking story at dusk. That's the whole point.

And that's exactly why this page exists. Consent here is a living door — it opens both ways. You can be in the archive today and gone from it tomorrow. You can be in by camp consensus today and add your own personal yes — or your own personal no — any day after. Nothing on this site — no clip, no photo, no transcript, no good story — is worth more than a single person's comfort.

⚙️What happens when you email "remove"

  1. The media comes down — video, photo, audio, and its transcript pulled from the site.
  2. The ledger is stamped — the item's consent flips to revoked, with a timestamp. The decision is remembered so it's never quietly re-published.
  3. The search index is purged — your name and words stop surfacing.
  4. Frames and audio are purged — extracted stills, thumbnails, and audio rips go too. Not just the front door — the whole house.
  5. You get a confirmation email back — a human tells you it's done.

Target: within 24 hours of your email, usually much faster.
